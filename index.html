Most online PDF tools require you to upload your sensitive documents to their servers. We believe your files are private. That's why PDF Studio processes everything directly in your web browser. Your files never leave your computer or phone.

About PDF Studio

PDF Studio was created by a student developer who wanted to build a fast, free, and secure alternative to traditional PDF tools. Most online tools upload your private files to a server, which can take time and compromise your privacy.

We built this tool using modern web technologies (JavaScript and PDF-lib), allowing your browser to do all the heavy lifting. This means no uploads, no waiting, and complete privacy.

Our goal is to keep this tool 100% free for everyone by using non-intrusive advertising.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024

At PDF Studio, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by PDF Studio and how we use it.

File Processing

Unlike other services, we do not upload your files to our servers. All PDF processing (merging, splitting, rotating) happens entirely within your web browser. We do not have access to your documents, and they are never stored on our servers.

Log Files and Cookies

PDF Studio follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

Google AdSense and Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use information (not including your name, address, email address, or telephone number) about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you.
